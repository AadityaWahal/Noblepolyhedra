Explore all 146 noble polyhedra organized by symmetry and orbit group in interactive 3D WebGL. Discover isogonal and isohedral 3D solid figures, facet geometry, and continuous deformation families.

Noble Polyhedra by Group

  • Group C (Cubic Orbit): Classical regular hexahedron (C-1) with octahedral symmetry.
  • Group D (Dodecahedral Rigid Orbit): 7 classical dodecahedral noble polyhedra (D-1 to D-7).
  • Group I (Icosahedral Rigid Orbit): 4 icosahedral solid shapes (I-1 to I-4).
  • Group ID (Icosidodecahedral Orbit): 6 edge-transitive polyhedra (ID-1 to ID-6).
  • Group O (Octahedral Orbit): Regular octahedron (O-1).
  • Group T (Tetrahedral Orbit): Regular tetrahedron (T-1).
  • Group rC & rD (Rectified Orbits): Rectified cubic (rC-1) and 19 rectified dodecahedral shapes (rD-1 to rD-19).
  • Group tC, tD, tI, tO (Truncated Orbits): Truncated cubic (tC-1), dodecahedral (tD-1 to tD-6), icosahedral fullerene-like shapes (tI-1 to tI-17), and octahedral (tO-1).
  • Group gC & gD (1-DOF Continuous Families): 3 cubic (gC-1 to gC-3) and 38 dodecahedral (gD-1 to gD-38) 1-parameter noble solids.
  • Group sC & sD (2-DOF Stevanović Discovery Families): 7 cubic (sC-1 to sC-7) and 33 dodecahedral (sD-1 to sD-33) modern 2-parameter continuous noble polyhedra.
